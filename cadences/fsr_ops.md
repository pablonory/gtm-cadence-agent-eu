**Persona brief (from `knowledge/`, shown to the rep at the top):**
A multi-site full-service ops lead is measured on labour cost %, GP per site, consistency site-to-site,
and hours lost to admin. Pain: complex rotas + compliance across large front- and back-of-house teams,
built manually ("a waste of my time, it's not profit leading"), so labour balance and standards drift
from one site to the next. Nory's answer: the compliant schedule built against a live demand forecast
in seconds, the same way at every site — and one place to see all of them. Proof pulled from the
`proof_library.md` FSR set per send (never fabricated).

## Discovery goals (probe on the first live conversation — not in the cold email)
- **Incumbent + contract end date** — contract expiry is the #1 compelling event (`_signal_correlation.md`).
- **POS fit** — confirm early; a POS mismatch is a common DQ.
- **Who owns the rota today** — GM per site, or central ops? Surfaces the manual-Excel pain and the
  objection #5 ("GM rotas need judgment") before it lands (`_objections.md`).
