**Persona brief (from `knowledge/`, shown to the rep at the top):**
A QSR C-suite buyer owns growth, unit economics, and the investor story — measured on net profit, growth
rate, and the ability to scale without losing margin discipline. Pain: adding sites fast means variance
creeps venue to venue and the group margin quietly erodes, labour runs hot ("we've almost given up trying
to control labour"), and they're betting on luck rather than a system. Nory's answer: one OS that holds
margin discipline across every new site — the top three P&L lines in one live view, variance controlled as
the estate grows. Proof pulled from the `proof_library.md` **QSR set** per send (7Bone, Eat a Pitta,
Rocksalt), plus peer/logo credibility — never fabricated, never a coffee/FSR number.

## Discovery goals (probe on the first live conversation — not in the cold email)
- **Are they the decision-maker?** Franchise trap: ask directly "are you the right person, or does this
  sit at group/brand level?" (`_objections.md` #4) — the national/brand DM is rarely on the first call.
- **Incumbent + contract end date** — contract expiry is the #1 compelling event (`_signal_correlation.md`),
  surfaced on the call, never a first-touch hook.
- **POS fit** — confirm early; QSR carries the most POS-integration DQs (`context/icp/verticals.md`).
- If on **XtraChef / an inventory-first tool** → lead the displacement story (`proof_library.md` US section).
