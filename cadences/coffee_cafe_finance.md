**Persona brief (from `knowledge/`, shown to the rep at the top):**
A multi-site coffee/café finance lead is measured on margin per site, month-end close speed, and forecast
accuracy. Pain: labour % swings on unpredictable AM/daypart peaks, milk/bean/fresh COGS and prep waste
erode thin margins, both are visible only in arrears, and two figures rarely reconcile ("which number is
right?"). Every new café multiplies the blind spot. Nory's answer: one accurate, live P&L per site (GP,
stock, labour) scheduled to 15-min forecast demand, and a **Labour Assessment** that quantifies the
recoverable **$** up front. Proof pulled from the `proof_library.md` Coffee/Café set per send — proof-light,
never fabricated.

## Discovery goals (probe on the first live conversation — not in the cold email)
- **Incumbent + contract end date** — contract expiry is the #1 compelling event (`_signal_correlation.md`).
- **POS fit** — confirm early; a POS mismatch is a common DQ.
- **Pain shape (labour vs COGS)** — decides which proof resolves: forecasting-accuracy for labour/peaks, a COGS proof only if pain = GP/over-ordering (a UK group cut milk spend materially with purchasing insights — lead with the story/%, model the prospect's $, don't quote the UK £).
- If on **XtraChef / an inventory-first tool** → lead the displacement story (`proof_library.md` US section).
