**Persona brief (from `knowledge/`, shown to the rep at the top):**
A multi-site QSR ops lead is measured on labour cost %, GP per site, consistency across venues, and the
admin hours they lose. Pain: throughput-driven scheduling done in Excel "to the minute", the till not
talking to the labour system, and variance creeping as sites multiply — "I shouldn't be spending hours
manually figuring this out." Nory's answer: schedule to a 15-min demand forecast, control variance
across sites, and see every site in one place. Proof pulled from the `proof_library.md` QSR set per
send (never fabricated), matched to vertical only.

## Discovery goals (probe on the first live conversation — not in the cold email)
- **POS fit** — confirm early. **QSR carries the most disqualifications, incl. POS-integration DQs** —
  qualify POS fit before investing the cadence (`context/icp/verticals.md`).
- **Incumbent + contract end date** — contract expiry is the #1 compelling event (`_signal_correlation.md`).
- If on **XtraChef / an inventory-first tool** → lead the displacement story (`proof_library.md` US section).
