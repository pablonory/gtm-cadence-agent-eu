**Persona brief (from `knowledge/`, shown to the rep at the top):**
A multi-site QSR finance lead is measured on prime cost %, margin per site, close speed, and forecast
accuracy. Pain: labour % and variance are visible only in arrears, two figures rarely reconcile ("which
number is right?"), and every new counter multiplies the blind spot. Nory's answer: one accurate, live
P&L per site (GP, stock, labour), and a **Labour Assessment** that quantifies the recoverable **$** up
front. The QSR proof lets us lead with a real double — a similar brand pulled COL down and GP up in one
quarter. Proof pulled from the `proof_library.md` QSR set per send (never fabricated).

## Discovery goals (probe on the first live conversation — not in the cold email)
- **Incumbent + contract end date** — contract expiry is the #1 compelling event (`_signal_correlation.md`).
- **POS fit** — confirm early; QSR carries the most POS-integration DQs (`context/icp/verticals.md`).
- If on **XtraChef / an inventory-first tool** → lead the displacement story (`proof_library.md` US section).

### Email 1 — Day 1 — `CUSTOM` (agent generates per account)

> **Subject:** 3 new sites + the month-end scramble
>
> Hi {{first}} — saw you've opened 3 sites recently. Across that many counters labour % and portion

>
> We give QSR groups one live P&L per site, so the drift shows up the week it happens.
>
> Worth 20 minutes? {{rep_first}}

### Email 2 — Day 3 — templated (the accuracy angle)

### Email 3 — Day 9 — templated (proof — the QSR double)

> *(resolves from `proof_library.md` QSR set — the COL% + GP% double, e.g. 7Bone −13.5% COL / +3.3% GP, or Rocksalt −7% COL. Never fabricated.)*

### Email 4 — Day 13 — templated (the Labour Assessment — the finance ROI tool)
> **Subject:** a modelled number before you commit anything
> Hi {{first}} — rather than pitch, we can run a quick **Labour Assessment**: from your current labour %
> and site count we model the **$** you'd recover, before any commercials. Want us to run yours? {{rep_first}}
> *(the ROI-tangibility mechanism from `_objections.md` — finance wants a **$** number, not "save time". Model it on the prospect's own costs; don't quote a UK £ figure as a US $.)*

### Email 5 — Day 19 — templated (breakup)
