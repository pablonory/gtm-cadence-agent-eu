**Persona brief (from `knowledge/`, shown to the rep at the top):**
A full-service C-suite owner is measured on net profit, growth rate, and the ability to scale without
losing margin discipline — and on the story they tell the board/investors. Pain: labour % creeping past
target across the estate ("tracking 39%, need 35%"), every cost line up at once, and growth that leans on
luck and a patchwork of tools rather than repeatable unit economics. Nory's answer: one OS that holds
margin discipline site-by-site so expansion is safe — live P&L per site, prime-cost control, net profit
protected as the estate grows. Proof pulled from the `proof_library.md` FSR set per send (never fabricated).

## Discovery goals (probe on the first live conversation — not in the cold email)
- **Decision process** — "Who else needs to be in this before a decision?" C-suite deals need the seat
  mapped early (`_objections.md` #4); Owner/C-suite decides, Finance validates, Ops champions.
- **Incumbent + contract end date** — contract expiry is the #1 compelling event (`_signal_correlation.md`);
  surfaces on the call, never a cold-email hook.
- **POS fit** — confirm early; a POS mismatch is a common DQ.
- If on **XtraChef / an inventory-first tool** → lead the displacement story (`proof_library.md` US section).

## Flow
Compresses the default `_flow_structure.md` sequence to **10 touches over ~15 business days** (Email 1 =
`CUSTOM`, rest templated). C-suite get fewer, sharper touches — no five-email drip.

| Day | Channel | Touch | Purpose |
|----|---------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Email | Email 1 | **`CUSTOM`** — bespoke first touch (signals × knowledge) |
| 1 | LinkedIn | Connect | connection request, no pitch |
| 2 | Call | Call 1 | templated opener |
| 4 | Email | Email 2 | templated — scale-without-margin-erosion angle |
| 6 | LinkedIn | Message 1 | templated — reference the email + one proof |
| 8 | Call | Call 2 + VM | templated + voicemail |
| 11 | Email | Email 3 | templated — peer/proof point |
| 13 | Call | Call 3 | templated |
| 15 | Email | Email 4 | templated — breakup |

Channel mix: **4 email · 3 call · 2 LinkedIn · 1 voicemail**.

### Email 1 — Day 1 — `CUSTOM` (agent generates per account)
Example for an account with signals *funding raised + 3 openings planned*:
> **Subject:** scaling to 10 sites without labour creeping
>
> Hi {{first}} — saw you've raised and you're opening 3 more this year. That's usually the point where
> labour % starts drifting across the estate and net margin slips as you add sites, even when the flagship
> looks fine.
>
> We give full-service groups one live P&L per site, so the economics hold as you scale — not just at the
> best restaurant.
>
> Worth 20 minutes? {{rep_first}}

### Email 2 — Day 4 — templated (scale without margin erosion)
> **Subject:** adding sites without adding risk
> Hi {{first}} — most full-service groups your size can run one great site on instinct. Holding the same
> margin across ten, with every cost line up, is the hard part. We give you unit economics you can see per
> site, so growth runs on a system instead of luck. Worth a short conversation? {{rep_first}}

### Email 3 — Day 11 — templated (peer proof)
> **Subject:** {{fsr_proof.brand}} — {{fsr_proof.metric}}
> Hi {{first}} — {{fsr_proof.brand}} pulled cost of labour down {{fsr_proof.result}} while scaling, similar
> setup to yours. We're also live with US groups like Ark Restaurants. Happy to show how the margin holds
> as sites go on. 20 min? {{rep_first}}
> *(resolves from `proof_library.md` FSR set — **lead with the %**: Passyunk −26% COL, Jamie Oliver −9%,
> Grounded Kitchen −4.8%, Masa −9.7%. The £/€ absolutes are UK/EU actuals — never relabel as $. Ark/Heirloom
> are **logo credibility only** — never quote their deal value as a saving.)*

### Email 4 — Day 15 — templated (breakup)
> **Subject:** closing the loop
> Hi {{first}} — haven't caught you, so I'll assume protecting margin as you scale isn't the priority this
> quarter and close this out. If that changes as the estate grows, I'm one reply away. {{rep_first}}
