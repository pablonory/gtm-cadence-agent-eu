**Persona brief (from `knowledge/`, shown to the rep at the top):**
A multi-site full-service finance lead is measured on margin per site, month-end close speed, and
forecast accuracy. Pain: labour % and COGS are visible only in arrears, two figures rarely reconcile
("which number is right?"), and P&L inaccuracies hit the quarter review. Nory's answer: one accurate,
live P&L per site (GP, stock, labour), and a **Labour Assessment** that quantifies the recoverable **$**
up front. Proof pulled from the `proof_library.md` FSR set per send (never fabricated).

## Discovery goals (probe on the first live conversation — not in the cold email)
- **Incumbent + contract end date** — contract expiry is the #1 compelling event (`_signal_correlation.md`).
- **POS fit** — confirm early; a POS mismatch is a common DQ.
- If on **XtraChef / an inventory-first tool** → lead the displacement story (`proof_library.md` US section).

### Email 1 — Day 1 — `CUSTOM` (agent generates per account)

> **Subject:** 2 new sites + the month-end scramble
>
> Hi {{first}} — saw you've opened 2 sites recently. That's usually when labour % and COGS start
> drifting across the estate, and finance only catches it at month-end once it's already banked.
>
> We give full-service groups one live P&L per site, so the drift shows up the week it happens.
>
> Worth 20 minutes? {{rep_first}}

### Email 2 — Day 3 — templated (the accuracy angle)
> **Subject:** which labour number is right?
> Hi {{first}} — most multi-site finance leads I speak to have two cost figures that don't quite agree,
> and spend half of every month working out which to trust. Nory gives you one accurate live number per
> site — GP, stock, labour. Worth a quick look? {{rep_first}}

### Email 3 — Day 9 — templated (proof)
> **Subject:** {{fsr_proof.brand}} — {{fsr_proof.metric}}
> Hi {{first}} — {{fsr_proof.brand}} pulled cost of labour down {{fsr_proof.result}} after getting live
> per-site visibility — similar setup to yours. Happy to show how. Worth 20 min? {{rep_first}}
> *(resolves from `proof_library.md` FSR set — lead with the **%**, e.g. Passyunk −26% COL or Grounded Kitchen −4.8% COL. The £/€ absolutes are UK/EU actuals — never relabel as $, never fabricated.)*

### Email 4 — Day 13 — templated (the Labour Assessment — the finance ROI tool)
> **Subject:** a modelled number before you commit anything
> Hi {{first}} — rather than pitch, we can run a quick **Labour Assessment**: from your current labour %
> and site count we model the **$** you'd recover, before any commercials. Want us to run yours? {{rep_first}}
> *(the ROI-tangibility mechanism from `_objections.md` — finance wants a **$** number, not "save time". Model it on the prospect's own costs; don't quote a UK £ figure as a US $.)*

### Email 5 — Day 19 — templated (breakup)
