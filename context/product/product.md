## What Nory is
AI-native **Restaurant Operating System** that automates and controls **prime costs (COGS + labour)**
for multi-location hospitality operators. A "crew of AI assistants" (agentic AI) that forecast
demand, build schedules, manage ordering/inventory, and analyse reviews — autonomously, across every
site. Brand line: **"No profit lost." / "Operational Intelligence."**

Positioned as an **operating system, not a point solution** — one platform replacing a patchwork of
scheduling tools, spreadsheets, manual payroll, and disconnected inventory systems.

## Belief system (the POV outbound should carry)
- Restaurants shouldn't **rely on luck** to stay open — they need better systems.
- The enemy is **operational inefficiency, waste & guesswork**, never the operator.
- Hospitality doesn't need *more* tools — it needs **one AI-native operating system**.
- Profit lost to inefficiency is profit **recoverable**.
