# Magnus's best cold outbound — the worked example (captured 2026-09-18)

Shared unprompted in his survey #2 reply (Pablo's Slack DM, ts 1789729895.021929) as the thing he
measures outbound against. Kept verbatim because it is the only example we have from the rep who
rejected the shared company flows as "AI slop", so it is the closest thing to a target we have.

**Context he gave:** cold, to a CTO. "It name drops early to make it feel warmer. It's highly specific
to the research we could only have got from his colleagues."

## What the agent can copy from this

- **Reason for writing, before any fact.** Two lines in, Darren knows who sent him here and why this
  email exists. This is the fix for the "starts mid-sentence" tell (`anti_ai_writing_style.md` §4).
- **A named, checkable customer early** ("we work with Sticks n Sushi… the other McWin brands"). The
  agent already picks a matched customer per account; this is where it belongs — near the top, as
  context, not at the bottom as a proof stat.
- **The ask is soft and self-aware**: *"let me know the least annoying time to touch base"*. Register to
  borrow directly. Compare our current close, "Worth 20 minutes?", which is fine but flatter.
- **First-person experience carries the credibility**, not a percentage. The monday.com/BigBrain
  paragraph is him, not a case study, and it concedes a limit ("certain problems were better solved
  externally") — a concession is the most persuasive move in the email and the one AI copy never makes.
- **A P.S. with a genuine human detail.** Costs one line, does more warmth work than the rest combined.

## What the agent structurally cannot copy — say so, don't fake it

- **"Tom Hatcher… pointed me in your direction"** and **"Understand from Nick that…"** come from
  colleague relationships and internal conversations. There is no public source for either. The agent
  must never manufacture this kind of line: a fabricated referral is the single most damaging thing it
  could put in a rep's email. Where a real internal referral exists, the rep adds it; the agent leaves
  the room for it.
- **The wife/CFA P.S.** is his own life. Not portable, and not to be imitated with invented detail.

## The rule this produces

The agent's job on an account like this is the middle layer — the dated, checkable, account-specific
fact and the matched customer — wrapped in a structure that leaves the top (referral, internal context)
and the bottom (personal P.S.) for the rep to fill. Write the email so those slots are natural to add,
not so they have to be bolted on.
