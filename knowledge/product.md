## The one-liner (for outbound)
Nory is an **AI-native restaurant operating system** that controls prime cost — **COGS + labour** —
across every site, so multi-location operators stop losing margin to guesswork and month-end blind
spots. "No profit lost."

## What it actually does (in plain terms)
- **Forecasts demand** every 15 min per site (~97% accuracy) → the base layer everything else runs on.
- **Builds optimal schedules** in seconds against that forecast, budget, and compliance → labour cost
  under control before the week starts.
- **Manages ordering + inventory** against forecast demand → ~50% less waste, auto supplier POs.
- **Analyses reviews** and links them back to ops data.
- **Live P&L per site** — the finance headline: see labour % and COGS the week they happen, not at
  month-end.
- Coming 2026: Compliance, Payroll, Invoicing. Plus **Nory Capital** financing.

## The category wedge
One operating system **replaces the patchwork** (scheduling app + spreadsheets + manual payroll +
disconnected inventory). Plugs into existing POS (Toast, Square, Clover) + payroll/accounts. Open API.

## The economic argument (the spine of every pitch)
Prime cost (COGS + labour) is the **two biggest controllable cost lines**. On 3–5% net margins,
operators lose **£60K–120K per £1M revenue/year** to prime-cost leakage — 100–200% of net profit.
Nory recovers that. This is the number that makes finance and C-suite lean in.
