Rules of use

  • Quantify or fall back. No figure on their footprint → the entry is context; the email opens on the vertical pain instead.
  • Facts, never advice. Stating a rate change is fine; how to restructure staffing, tips or contracts is legal/tax advice.
  • No fear-mongering. The change and the date. Precision is the value, not alarm.
  • An account signal always wins. If Stage 1 found a real signal, open on that, not on macro.
