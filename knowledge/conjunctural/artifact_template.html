    Rules of use

    Before any of this reaches a prospect

    Quantify or fall back. If the page cannot render a figure on their footprint, the entry is context only — the email opens on the vertical pain instead.
    Facts, never advice. Stating a rate change is fine; suggesting how to restructure staffing, tips or contracts around it is legal/tax advice and not ours to give.
    No fear-mongering. State the change and the date. The prospect already knows their costs are rising; the value is precision, not alarm.
    An account signal always wins. This page is the fallback layer. If Stage 1 found a real signal on the account, open on that, not on macro.
