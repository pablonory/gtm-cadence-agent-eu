# Jobs-to-be-done by persona

> Stage 2a output, produced by `ga_jtbd_persona`. The job each persona is hired to do, what they're
> measured on, and the message frame that speaks to it. Feeds the persona brief at the top of each
> cadence cell + the first-touch angle. Grounded in `context/icp/personas.md`. Seeded v1.

## Ops — "keep every site running profitably and consistently"
- **JTBD:** deliver P&L across sites without firefighting; keep labour and stock under control; get
  admin hours back to spend on the floor.
- **Measured on:** labour cost %, GP per site, consistency site-to-site, admin hours.
- **Hire Nory to:** stop building rotas by hand, stop guessing orders, see every site in one place.
- **Message frame:** hours back + control at portfolio scale. Concrete, operational, low-effort
  rollout. Proof: labour −10–20%, 12.3 hrs saved/day, ~97% forecast.
- **Progress they want:** "I can run 10 more sites without 10x the chaos."

## Finance — "protect the margin and close faster with numbers I trust"
- **JTBD:** control prime cost, forecast reliably, close month-end faster, report margin per site to
  the board with confidence.
- **Measured on:** prime cost %, margin per site, close speed, forecast accuracy.
- **Hire Nory to:** see labour % + COGS live per site instead of in arrears; kill the month-end
  surprise; recover leaking margin.
- **Message frame:** live P&L per site, catch drift the week it happens. **Proof-led — every claim
  paired with a named brand + number.** Proof: £60K–120K/£1M recoverable, payroll 2 days → 1 hour.
- **Progress they want:** "I stop finding out about the leak four weeks too late."

## C-suite — "scale the group without losing margin discipline"
- **JTBD:** grow sites/revenue while protecting net profit and unit economics; tell a credible
  scale-and-margin story to investors; bet on systems over luck.
- **Measured on:** net profit, growth rate, unit economics, ability to scale.
- **Hire Nory to:** make expansion safe — one OS that holds margin discipline across every new site.
- **Message frame:** the category POV (control vs luck, "No profit lost"), portfolio economics, the
  strategic bet. Shortest, highest-altitude, peer/investor proof (funding, tier-1 logos, net profit
  +up to 50%). Proof: DHC +19.4% GP while scaling; 80% location growth + 50% variance reduction.
- **Progress they want:** "I can double the estate without doubling the risk."

## Real words (VOC anchors — from `gong_evidence/_voc.md`)
Use the buyer's own phrasing, not ours:
- **Ops:** "I shouldn't be spending hours manually figuring this out — it's a waste of my time, it's
  not profit leading." Manual Excel/"GPT sheets", till not talking to the labour system.
- **Finance:** "there's a difference of one or two percent, so I always get 'which one is right?'" —
  inaccurate GP/stocks/labour hitting the P&L and quarter reviews.
- **C-suite:** "labour is without doubt our biggest challenge — tracking 39%, need 35%." Knows the
  number cold; every cost line is up.

first touch leads with the persona's JTBD, sharpened by the account's strongest Stage 1 signal, and
echoes the persona's real words above.
