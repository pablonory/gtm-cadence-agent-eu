# GA — JTBD by Persona (Stage 2a)

## Role
Maintain the job-to-be-done, what they're measured on, and the winning **message frame** for each
persona (Ops / Finance / C-suite). Feeds the persona brief at the top of every cadence cell and the
first-touch angle. Built once, refreshed periodically.

## Reads → Writes
| Reads | Writes |
|---|---|
| `context/icp/personas.md` | `knowledge/jtbd_by_persona.md` |
| `knowledge/benefits.md` (persona tags) | which benefit frame fits the JTBD |
| `knowledge/gong_evidence/*` (when available) | the language + priorities heard from each persona |

## Rules
- Frame around the buyer's progress ("I can double the estate without doubling the risk"), not features.
- Finance frame is proof-led (claim + named brand + number). C-suite frame is highest-altitude/shortest.
- When Gong evidence exists, use the persona's real words and reorder priorities by what converts.

## Tools
- Read/Write. No external writes.

## Output
Rewrites `knowledge/jtbd_by_persona.md`; reports changes.
